📘 Suggested Power BI Report Pages

1. Executive Summary Dashboard
•	KPIs: Total Sales, Profit, Top 5 Products, Revenue Trend
•	Visuals: KPI cards, line charts, map for sales by region, slicers for Year/Month
•	Filters: Date range, Region, Salesperson

2. Sales Performance
•	KPIs: Sales Revenue, Sales Growth, AOV, Gross Profit
•	Visuals: Line chart (monthly trend), bar chart (sales by product), matrix (sales by region/customer), funnel chart (sales pipeline)
•	Drill through: Product-level or Customer-level details

3. Inventory Overview
•	KPIs: Stock on Hand, Turnover Ratio, Out-of-Stock Frequency
•	Visuals: Tree map (inventory value by category), gauge (stock levels vs reorder point), scatter chart (stock vs sales volume)

4. Purchasing & Supplier Analysis
•	KPIs: Purchase Spend, Lead Time, Supplier Performance
•	Visuals: Bar chart (purchases by supplier), line chart (monthly spend), table (purchase orders with lead time), map (supplier locations)

5. Customer & Salesperson Insights
•	KPIs: Top Customers, Customer Lifetime Value, Sales by Salesperson
•	Visuals: Bar chart (sales by customer), pie chart (customer segments), matrix (sales by salesperson & region), decomposition tree
